Digital media products, built for the rhythm of sport.

Explore our work → About the studio

Totalizator Sportowy Media is a Warsaw-based studio focused on the craft of building reliable, well-designed digital products for the sports media ecosystem — from native mobile apps and content surfaces to internal tooling for editorial teams.

What we build

Three product families, one engineering philosophy.

Our work clusters around the surfaces sports audiences live in every day — phones, dashboards, and editorial workflows.

Mobile Media Applications

Native and cross-platform apps that deliver news, results, schedules, and editorial coverage to large audiences without compromise on performance.

Audience Engagement Surfaces

Companion experiences, fan hubs, and matchday surfaces that turn passive viewers into engaged readers — built around clarity and respect for attention.

Data & Editorial Tooling

Analytics dashboards and publishing workflows that give editorial teams the visibility, speed, and structure they need to ship coverage on a tight news cycle.

Privacy-conscious by design

Data minimisation is a default, not an afterthought. We build products that respect users from the first tap onward.

Responsible publishing

Editorial integrity, transparent sources, and a clear separation between journalism and promotion are core to how our platforms operate.

Built to scale

Cloud-native architecture and modern delivery pipelines keep our products fast and reliable under matchday load.
