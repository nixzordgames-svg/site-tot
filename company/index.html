Company

A studio focused on sports media products.

Totalizator Sportowy Media is a Warsaw-based technology and media company. We design, build, and maintain digital products that serve sports audiences, editorial teams, and the partners who work with them.

Who we are

An independent product studio with a clear focus.

Our team brings together engineers, designers, and editorial-minded product people who share a long-standing interest in how sport is consumed, discussed, and reported in the digital age.

We build software for the surfaces where this happens — mobile, web, and the tools that power the people behind the headlines.

At a glance

  • Headquartered in Warsaw, Poland
  • Focused exclusively on media and information products
  • Cross-functional engineering and design teams
  • European operating footprint
  • Active across mobile, web, and editorial tooling
Mission

Build sports media products that earn the audience's trust.

We believe digital sports media should inform first, perform second, and respect the reader always. That principle guides what we ship and what we decline to ship.

Long-term direction

We're investing in product depth rather than short-term experiments — building a portfolio of media platforms that we expect to operate and improve for years.

What we craft

Sports news applications, audience engagement surfaces, statistics dashboards, and the editorial tooling that supports the people producing daily coverage.

How we work

Small, focused teams with strong design and engineering disciplines. Honest scoping, clear ownership, and a strong preference for shipping incrementally.

European footprint

Our team operates from Warsaw, working within the legal and regulatory expectations of the European Union and the Republic of Poland.

Editorial-aware engineering

We design our platforms in close dialogue with editorial workflows, so the technology supports the people producing coverage rather than constraining them.

Consistent principles

Honest scope, careful release management, and a strong bias toward products we'd be proud to use ourselves.
