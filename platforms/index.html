Platforms

Product categories we design & operate.

Every platform we build is informational and media-oriented. We do not produce, host, or facilitate transactional products tied to match outcomes of any kind.

Sports news applications

Editorial-led mobile and web apps delivering breaking news, long-form features, opinion, and analysis — structured around a clean reading experience and durable archive.

Live content & matchday hubs

Real-time text commentary, lineup pages, schedules, and editorial coverage that surfaces alongside fixtures — designed to be calm and readable under heavy traffic.

Companion apps for fans

Personalised reading surfaces, club and league hubs, push-notification routing, and the editorial features that turn a casual visitor into a returning reader.

Analytics & statistics dashboards

Informational dashboards summarising league standings, team form, historical results, and other publicly available data sources, presented in a clear and accessible format.

Tooling for publishers & editorial teams

Internal CMS-adjacent surfaces, asset management, scheduling tools, and dashboards that give editorial teams better visibility over what they're producing.

Public-information products

Lightweight reference surfaces — rule explainers, glossaries, historical archives — that complement primary editorial coverage with durable, evergreen material.

Scope of our platforms. All products described on this page are informational and media-oriented. Totalizator Sportowy Media does not develop, distribute, or operate transactional sports products of any kind.
